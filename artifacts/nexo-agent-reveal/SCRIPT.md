# NexoAI — 6-Second Reveal

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** One sharp hit of the Nexo mark, then the complete Agent Mode identity.
- **Identity:** Use only the supplied splash artwork; preserve its near-black, cobalt, and white palette and never redraw the logo.
- **Hero:** The original symbol, first as a close crop and then as part of the complete splash.
- **Motion:** Split-shutter snap, two tight flash beats, then a smooth opacity dissolve into the full splash.
- **Arc:** Impact → logo afterimage → the same centered mark softly resolves into the Agent Mode artwork.
- **Payoff:** The supplied NexoAI / Agent Mode artwork resolves cleanly and remains visible at the end.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape. **Runtime:** 6 seconds, compressed from 14 for a fast, high-impact ad.
- **Audio:** None. No soundtrack, voiceover, sound effects, or audio player.

## Full shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.00–1.80s / 1.80s — “THE HIT.”
- **Purpose:** Stop the scroll immediately with the recognizable symbol, then create anticipation with a short rhythmic interruption.
- **Exact content:** Only a macro crop of the original Nexo symbol from `public/images/nexo-agent-splash.png`. No added text, captions, or graphic lines.
- **Composition:** The splash image is enlarged 3.3x around the exact symbol with a fixed 50%/22% transform origin; the built-in wordmark and Agent Mode pill remain outside the crop. At frame one, two near-black shutters leave a narrow center slit over the bright mark.
- **Artistic idea:** Treat the logo like a camera flash afterimage: two brief near-black beats puncture a still, precisely centered mark before it dissolves into its original source image.
- **Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.18s: shutters snap outward, revealing the already-present symbol. 0.18–0.36s: image drops to a near-black afterimage. 0.36–0.54s: the mark snaps bright at the same scale. 0.54–0.68s: second short blackout. 0.68–1.12s: the brightest flash lands. 1.12–1.80s: hold the mark completely still and fully visible; at 1.80s begin the crossfade.
- **Transition contract:** Both shots use the same original splash image and keep the symbol center fixed at 50%/22%. The macro image does not travel or bounce; as it fades away, the uncropped splash fades in beneath it, revealing the matching symbol at its natural size and the Agent Mode artwork around it.
- **Assets and technique:** Exact supplied `public/images/nexo-agent-splash.png`, transform-only crop held at a fixed scale, paired shutters, and opacity keyframes. The supplied MP4 is not used.
- **Sound intent:** Silence.

### Shot 02 / 1.80–6.00s / 4.20s — “THE REVEAL.”
- **Purpose:** Pay off the symbol tease with the full product identity, ending on the supplied splash artwork.
- **Exact content:** The original artwork's “NexoAI” wordmark and “Agent Mode” label are part of the supplied image. No added copy, CTA, caption, or slogan.
- **Composition:** Exact `public/images/nexo-agent-splash.png`, uncropped at 16:9; retain the full symbol, wordmark, Agent Mode capsule, and original corner artwork.
- **Artistic idea:** The macro logo dissolves into the exact same mark as printed in its source image; the surrounding product identity arrives through the same fade, not a camera move.
- **Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.55s: fade the uncropped splash from transparent to fully visible while the outgoing macro fades away. The logo center stays fixed; only its apparent scale changes through the overlapping images. 0.55–4.20s: hold the complete splash still and fully visible.
- **Transition contract:** The full splash is a static, unmodified image with the logo at the same center point as shot 01. The two scene layers crossfade over 0.55s; no scale, position, spring, shutter, wipe, redraw, or added line is used in the transition. The final frame is the complete splash.
- **Assets and technique:** Exact supplied `public/images/nexo-agent-splash.png`, one static full-frame image fading in; no recreated logo artwork.
- **Sound intent:** Silence.

## Variety, pacing, and continuity review
- The mark stays centered while the macro fades into its matching natural-size version inside the full artwork; no bounce or spring motion is used.
- The two short black beats are intentional and under 0.2s each; the hero remains visible at every handoff.
- No added blue graphic lines, slogans, copy, or product claims. The original blue details inside the final splash remain untouched.
- Total runtime is 6 seconds; the final frame is the full supplied Agent Mode artwork.
