# NexoAI — 6-Second Reveal

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** Guide the Nexo mark upward into place, then reveal the complete Agent Mode identity.
- **Identity:** Use only the supplied splash artwork; preserve its near-black, cobalt, and white palette and never redraw the logo.
- **Hero:** The original symbol, first as a close crop and then as part of the complete splash.
- **Motion:** One controlled bottom-up rise, followed by a soft opacity dissolve that starts on the opening logo and ends on the full splash.
- **Arc:** The opening mark rises into its final position → it stays visible as the original Agent Mode artwork fades in around it.
- **Payoff:** The supplied NexoAI / Agent Mode artwork resolves cleanly and remains visible at the end.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape. **Runtime:** 6 seconds, compressed from 14 for a fast, high-impact ad.
- **Audio:** None. No soundtrack, voiceover, sound effects, or audio player.

## Full shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.00–1.80s / 1.80s — “THE RISE.”
- **Purpose:** Draw the eye upward with the recognizable Nexo mark, then align it with the logo position in the final artwork.
- **Exact content:** Only a macro crop of the original Nexo symbol from `public/images/nexo-agent-splash.png`. No added text, captions, or graphic lines.
- **Composition:** The splash image is enlarged 3.3x around the exact symbol with a fixed 50%/35% transform origin; the built-in wordmark and Agent Mode pill remain outside the crop. The logo starts 32% below its final vertical anchor.
- **Artistic idea:** The same source-image mark rises in one clean, unbroken move to its exact location in the final splash; the full product image then dissolves around it.
- **Timed choreography:** 0.00s: the macro mark is already visible, fixed at 3.3x and 32% below its final anchor. 0.00–1.55s: it travels smoothly upward to `y: 0%` with a gentle ease-out and no scale or opacity pulse. 1.55–1.80s: hold the aligned opening logo perfectly still and fully visible. At 1.80s begin the crossfade directly from that logo.
- **Transition contract:** Both shots use the same original splash image and keep the symbol center fixed at 50%/35%. The macro's upward move ends before the cut. The opening logo itself is the first visible layer in the dissolve; the uncropped splash fades in around its matching logo, with no blank or black frame, flash, bounce, or spring.
- **Assets and technique:** Exact supplied `public/images/nexo-agent-splash.png`, transform-only crop, fixed scale, and vertical `y` translation. The supplied MP4 is not used.
- **Sound intent:** Silence.

### Shot 02 / 1.80–6.00s / 4.20s — “THE REVEAL.”
- **Purpose:** Pay off the symbol tease with the full product identity, ending on the supplied splash artwork.
- **Exact content:** The original artwork's “NexoAI” wordmark and “Agent Mode” label are part of the supplied image. No added copy, CTA, caption, or slogan.
- **Composition:** Exact `public/images/nexo-agent-splash.png`, uncropped at 16:9; retain the full symbol, wordmark, Agent Mode capsule, and original corner artwork.
- **Artistic idea:** The mark reaches its destination first; the complete original product image then appears around it as a gentle crossfade.
- **Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.55s: linearly fade the uncropped splash from transparent to fully visible over the still-visible opening logo; the outgoing macro fades away in the same interval. No image scale or position changes during this transition. 0.55–4.20s: hold the complete splash still and fully visible.
- **Transition contract:** The full splash is a static, unmodified image with the logo at the same center point as shot 01. The two scene layers crossfade linearly over 0.55s, starting on the opening logo rather than a blank field; no scale, bounce, spring, shutter, wipe, redraw, or added line is used. The final frame is the complete splash.
- **Assets and technique:** Exact supplied `public/images/nexo-agent-splash.png`, one static full-frame image fading in; no recreated logo artwork.
- **Sound intent:** Silence.

## Variety, pacing, and continuity review
- The macro mark moves upward once, stops exactly on the source artwork's logo position, then dissolves into its natural-size version; no flashing or bounce motion is used.
- The hero remains visible at every handoff. No added blue graphic lines, slogans, copy, or product claims are introduced; the original blue details inside the final splash remain untouched.
- Total runtime is 6 seconds; the final frame is the full supplied Agent Mode artwork.
