# NexoAI — 6-Second Reveal

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** One sharp hit of the Nexo mark, then the complete Agent Mode identity.
- **Identity:** Use only the supplied splash artwork; preserve its near-black, cobalt, and white palette and never redraw the logo.
- **Hero:** The original symbol, first as a close crop and then as part of the complete splash.
- **Motion:** Split-shutter snap, two tight flash beats, then a locked-scale pullback of the exact same artwork.
- **Arc:** Impact → logo afterimage → the very same mark resolves into the full Agent Mode splash.
- **Payoff:** The supplied NexoAI / Agent Mode artwork resolves cleanly and remains visible at the end.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape. **Runtime:** 6 seconds, compressed from 14 for a fast, high-impact ad.
- **Audio:** None. No soundtrack, voiceover, sound effects, or audio player.

## Full shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.00–1.80s / 1.80s — “THE HIT.”
- **Purpose:** Stop the scroll immediately with the recognizable symbol, then create anticipation with a short rhythmic interruption.
- **Exact content:** Only a macro crop of the original Nexo symbol from `public/images/nexo-agent-splash.png`. No added text, captions, or graphic lines.
- **Composition:** The splash image is enlarged around the exact symbol; the built-in wordmark and Agent Mode pill remain outside the crop. At frame one, two near-black shutters leave a narrow center slit over the bright mark.
- **Artistic idea:** Treat the logo like a camera flash afterimage: two brief near-black beats fracture the initial image before a final push-through.
- **Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.18s: shutters snap outward, revealing the already-present symbol. 0.18–0.36s: image drops to a near-black afterimage. 0.36–0.54s: the mark snaps bright at a slightly tighter scale. 0.54–0.68s: second short blackout. 0.68–1.12s: the largest flash lands and holds. 1.12–1.80s: the bright symbol expands from 3.45x to 4.00x without fading, ending on its exact center and scale for the next shot.
- **Transition contract:** There is no visual jump in the mark: both shots use the same original splash image, 4.00x scale, 15% vertical offset, and 50%/35% transform origin at the cut. The next shot begins pulling back from that identical close crop, so the existing logo itself visibly becomes the logo on the full Agent Mode image.
- **Assets and technique:** Exact supplied `public/images/nexo-agent-splash.png`, transform-only crop, paired shutters, and opacity/scale keyframes. The supplied MP4 is not used.
- **Sound intent:** Silence.

### Shot 02 / 1.80–6.00s / 4.20s — “THE REVEAL.”
- **Purpose:** Pay off the symbol tease with the full product identity, ending on the supplied splash artwork.
- **Exact content:** The original artwork's “NexoAI” wordmark and “Agent Mode” label are part of the supplied image. No added copy, CTA, caption, or slogan.
- **Composition:** Exact `public/images/nexo-agent-splash.png`, uncropped at 16:9; retain the full symbol, wordmark, Agent Mode capsule, and original corner artwork.
- **Artistic idea:** The logo is not replaced by another logo: its exact pixels shrink back into their source image, revealing the original product splash around it.
- **Timed choreography:** At 0.00s the same splash image starts at 4.00x scale and 15% vertical offset, matching shot 01 exactly. 0.00–0.63s: the camera pulls back from 4.00x to 1.00x and returns the offset to 0%, revealing the wordmark, Agent Mode pill, and original corner art around the still-centered symbol. 0.63–3.35s: hold the complete splash legibly. 3.35–4.20s: a tiny 1.015x camera push resolves back to 1.00x, ending on the complete, unmodified artwork.
- **Transition contract:** The same mark occupies the same position and scale at the cut; only the outgoing scene's brief section fade overlaps the incoming image. The shared crop then pulls back with no shutter, wipe, redraw, or added line. The final frame is the complete splash.
- **Assets and technique:** Exact supplied `public/images/nexo-agent-splash.png`, full-frame image plane, matched scale and vertical offset across the cut; no recreated logo artwork.
- **Sound intent:** Silence.

## Variety, pacing, and continuity review
- The exact Nexo mark continues across the cut: same image, origin, scale, and position at shot 01's end and shot 02's start; only then does the wider Agent Mode artwork emerge.
- The two short black beats are intentional and under 0.2s each; the hero remains visible at every handoff.
- No added blue graphic lines, slogans, copy, or product claims. The original blue details inside the final splash remain untouched.
- Total runtime is 6 seconds; the final frame is the full supplied Agent Mode artwork.
