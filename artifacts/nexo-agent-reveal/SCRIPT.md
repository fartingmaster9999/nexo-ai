# Nexo Agent — “Before it moves”

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** Reveal Nexo Agent as a capable presence; let the viewer feel its arrival before showing the full identity.
- **Identity:** Preserve the exact supplied Nexo splash artwork and Agent Mode footage. Near-black, cobalt, and white; restrained light, no invented logo treatments.
- **Hero:** The user-provided four-second Agent Mode clip, followed by a macro-to-full-frame reveal of the exact supplied splash.
- **Motion:** Aperture-like shutters, a slow macro pull-back, then a sudden clean brand reveal.
- **Arc:** Almost-dark anticipation → working product comes into view → the symbol is recognized → full Nexo Agent signature.
- **Payoff:** The final artwork is the first complete view of the NexoAI wordmark and Agent Mode capsule. No additional slogans or captions.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape. **Runtime:** 24 seconds; unchanged from the original cut.
- **Audio:** None. No soundtrack, voiceover, sound effects, or audio player. Source video remains muted.

## Full shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.00–4.00s / 4.00s — “A SLIVER OF LIGHT.”
- **Purpose:** Create suspense and orient the eye toward the center without announcing the product in text.
- **Exact content:** No added words, labels, or captions. Only the supplied brand palette is echoed in light.
- **Composition:** Near-black field. A hairline cobalt reflection begins 1vmin wide at x=50%, y=48%; a faint partial ring sits behind it at 36% of the short edge. Negative space dominates. Light stays within the middle 80% of the frame.
- **Artistic idea:** A hidden object is suggested only by reflections on the edge of its outline; the viewer sees a presence before its identity.
- **Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.35s near-black field with a barely visible central ring. 0.35s one 0.18s horizontal glint crosses the center. 0.90s a short vertical stroke appears, making a partial cross, then fades by 1.25s. 1.55–2.25s the ring rotates a few degrees and its upper-right segment catches cobalt light. 2.65s a second, smaller glint answers from the opposite edge. 3.15–4.00s two black shutters close toward center around one remaining light seam; the seam scales slightly forward into the next scene.
- **Transition contract:** End on a narrow vertical line at center. The next scene opens on the same line as a gap between two full-frame black shutters, while a bright point is already visible behind it.
- **Assets and technique:** CSS borders and restrained transforms over near-black; no fabricated Nexo mark or new asset.
- **Sound intent:** Silence.

### Shot 02 / 4.00–10.00s / 6.00s — “THE PRODUCT, IN MOTION.”
- **Purpose:** Reveal the real Agent Mode experience instead of summarizing it with copy.
- **Exact content:** No added words, interface labels, or captions. Only content already present in the supplied clip.
- **Composition:** The supplied 16:9 clip is full-frame behind two 50%-width black shutters. It starts as a central slit, then fills the frame at native proportions. A thin cobalt edge catches the shutters but never covers the footage.
- **Artistic idea:** The first scene's seam becomes the gap through which the actual product is discovered.
- **Timed choreography:** 0.00s clip is already present and muted behind two panels; center seam glows. 0.05–0.85s shutters translate outward and disappear, revealing the clip as a full frame. 1.00s the clip reaches full scale and sharp focus. 1.80s one soft linear highlight passes across the frame, not across any added text. 2.70s a short camera push begins toward the clip's center. 4.00s the camera releases slightly to recenter a bright detail. 5.05–6.00s that detail grows through the lens; outgoing scene scales into the central point.
- **Transition contract:** The center highlight grows as the scene cuts. Scene 03 begins with the supplied splash image already present at extreme macro scale, anchored to its actual center symbol.
- **Assets and technique:** `public/video/nexo-agent-mode.mp4`; loop and mute the supplied footage; shutters are plain transform-animated panels.
- **Sound intent:** Silence; source video is muted.

### Shot 03 / 10.00–16.00s / 6.00s — “THE MARK, BEFORE THE NAME.”
- **Purpose:** Let the viewer recognize the actual Nexo symbol before revealing the wordmark and Agent Mode pill.
- **Exact content:** No additional text. The supplied splash is shown as an enlarged crop around its real white interlocking symbol; “NexoAI” and “Agent Mode” remain beyond the visible crop until shot 04.
- **Composition:** Full-frame image crop, anchored on the symbol around the upper center of the supplied artwork. At the beginning, the mark occupies roughly 80% of the frame short edge. At the end it recedes to about 60%, still cropped enough that the brand lockup is not yet readable.
- **Artistic idea:** Pull the camera back from a luminous crossing detail so it reads as a symbol before its complete context arrives.
- **Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.25s the bright point from shot 02 lands at the symbol's center. 0.30–1.20s the crop resolves from 3.2x to 2.8x, revealing the interlocking white strokes. 1.75s a restrained cobalt reflection tracks along one edge. 2.40–3.40s camera pulls back to 2.25x; the mark becomes legible as a single shape without exposing the wordmark. 4.20s two black shutters begin to close inward, leaving a central view of the symbol. 5.25–6.00s the shutters meet on a narrow vertical seam, then split outward as the full artwork starts to appear behind them.
- **Transition contract:** The center seam is carried into shot 04. A slight scale-through and paired shutters reveal the full splash at the same central alignment.
- **Assets and technique:** The exact `public/images/nexo-agent-splash.png`, enlarged and centered on the original mark; black transform-animated shutters.
- **Sound intent:** Silence.

### Shot 04 / 16.00–24.00s / 8.00s — “THE FULL REVEAL.”
- **Purpose:** Deliver the only complete view of the NexoAI identity and leave it readable.
- **Exact content:** Only the supplied artwork's embedded “NexoAI” wordmark and “Agent Mode” label. No overlays, captions, or new copy.
- **Composition:** Exact supplied 16:9 splash fills the frame with the complete logo, wordmark, and Agent Mode capsule visible. Its original near-black and cobalt composition remains unchanged.
- **Artistic idea:** The shutter seam opens onto the exact source image, changing a macro detail into a complete identity in one decisive reveal.
- **Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.75s the image scales from 1.08x to 1.00x while left and right shutters recede. 0.85–1.15s the logo settles into focus. 1.70s one restrained blue light lift around the supplied mark; no new geometry obscures it. 2.60s the artwork holds, completely readable. 6.70–8.00s left and right shutters travel inward toward a central seam, reducing the image to the same vertical glint that opens shot 01.
- **Transition contract:** Final seam matches shot 01's x=50% reflection. The full lockup remains visible for over five seconds before the loop handoff.
- **Assets and technique:** Exact `public/images/nexo-agent-splash.png`, full frame and uncropped; paired black shutters.
- **Sound intent:** Silence.

## Variety and floor review
- The film moves through three distinct visual scales: abstract glint, full-motion source footage, macro crop, then complete splash artwork.
- Text outside the supplied brand art has been removed. There are no slogans, labels, or decorative copy.
- Each shot has a visible change at least every second, and every boundary carries the central light seam or point.
- The full artwork is legible before the closing shutter motion; no intermediate shot spoils the identity reveal.
- No audio is present or played. Ratio and scene durations are preserved.
